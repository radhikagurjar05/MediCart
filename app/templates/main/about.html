About MediCart

The story behind your campus marketplace.

Our Mission

MediCart was born out of a simple frustration — there was no safe, dedicated platform for Medi-Caps University students to trade used items within their campus. WhatsApp groups were chaotic, general marketplaces felt unsafe, and good deals were lost in the noise.

We built MediCart to be the trusted marketplace exclusively for verified Medi-Caps students. No strangers, no scams, no shipping — just simple, safe campus trading.

Tech Stack

Frontend

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript

Backend

Python, Flask

Database

MySQL, SQLAlchemy

Auth

Google OAuth, Flask-Login

Key Features

  • Campus-verified user registration
  • Buy, sell, and exchange products
  • Smart search with category filters
  • Wishlist with save/unsave
  • Seller dashboard with Chart.js analytics
  • Admin moderation panel
  • Dark mode support
  • Fully responsive design
